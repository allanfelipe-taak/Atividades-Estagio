FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
═════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Q: Can we skip the 48-hour monitoring window?
A: Not recommended. Minimum 24 hours to catch integration issues.

Q: What if a Flow can't be deleted?
A: Use Metadata API, or contact Salesforce Support. Rare but possible.

Q: What if dry-run fails?
A: Don't proceed. Fix the issue, re-test dry-run, then deploy.

Q: How do we rollback if something breaks?
A: Salesforce automatic backup. Last resort (data integrity risk).

Q: Will users be impacted?
A: No. New objects already in use. Cleanup runs in background.

Q: What if all Flows are business-critical?
A: Recreate them with new English objects first, then proceed.
